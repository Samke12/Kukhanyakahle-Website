Do I need a complete solar system to get started?

Not necessarily. Your best solution depends on your electricity usage, what you need to keep running, your property and your budget. We can help you understand whether you need backup power, solar panels, battery storage or a combination.

How do I know what size solar system I need?

The right size depends on what you use electricity for and how much power you need. We look at your requirements before recommending a system rather than simply giving you a standard package.

Can solar help my home or small business become more energy independent?

Yes. A properly designed solar and battery solution can help you generate and store your own electricity, reducing your reliance on the grid. The level of energy independence depends on your usage, system size, sunlight and battery capacity.
